Good to know

What are HE and HE*?

Recent Nikon Z cameras can save raw photos in High Efficiency (HE) or High Efficiency* (HE*) mode. The files are smaller, but they use a newer compression, TicoRAW (based on JPEG XS), that many editors cannot read yet. DNG is an open raw format that almost every editor reads.

Files saved in Nikon's classic modes (lossless, lossy or uncompressed) need no conversion: your editor already opens them, and this page tells you so.

Which cameras work?

The Nikon Z 9, Z 8, Z f, Z6III, Z 5 II, Z 50 II and ZR. On public sample files from each of them, the raw image in the DNG is identical, bit for bit, to the one Adobe DNG Converter writes. Other cameras may work too; if yours is not listed, please tell us about it (opens in a new tab).

What is in the DNG?

The exact raw data your camera recorded, with its settings, lens, date, GPS position (if any), Nikon's own metadata and a preview. It is not a processed picture: your editor still does the demosaicing, white balance and colour, just as with the NEF.

How big a batch can I convert?

Your browser holds the converted files until you download them, so very large batches need plenty of free memory and disk space. Saving straight into a folder avoids that. For whole memory cards, the ticoraw command-line tool is faster.
